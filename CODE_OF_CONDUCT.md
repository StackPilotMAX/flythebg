# FlyThe BG Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want FlyThe BG to be useful, welcoming and technically serious. Contributors and community members should communicate respectfully, give constructive feedback, protect user privacy and treat accessibility and security concerns seriously.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Assume good faith while discussing disagreements.
- Give credit for work and ideas when credit is due.
- Report security or privacy problems responsibly.
- Keep user data, credentials and private files out of public issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hateful conduct, sexual harassment, doxxing, credential sharing, malicious disruption, impersonation, targeted abuse and deliberate exposure of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may moderate or remove content and restrict participation where necessary to keep the project usable and safe.

## Contact

For sensitive security or privacy issues, contact support@flythebg.com privately instead of posting credentials or exploitable details publicly.
